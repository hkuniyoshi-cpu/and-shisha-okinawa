About Us

A stop that turns
“in between” into the best part.

and shisha — the idea is simple: something and shisha. A break in the middle of a drive up the coast. The gap before hotel check-in. The wind-down after a dive or a beach day. We turn the in-between hours of your Okinawa trip into something you actually remember.

The space is calm and refined — as easy to walk into as a café. Come for a full session, or just for a coffee or a beer. First time with shisha? Never touched one? Our staff will guide you in English, and nicotine-free options mean there is no pressure at all.

Take a breath in between the fun.
